Contact: surfmarttrading@gmail.com

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use MasterLister, information processed by the service may include:

  • authentication-related information;
  • subscription information;
  • trial status;
  • connected eBay account information;
  • listing workflow data;
  • listing draft information;

When you connect an eBay seller account, MasterLister may receive information required to identify and operate the authorized connection.

MasterLister may process authorization credentials or tokens required by the supported eBay integration.

These credentials must not be intentionally displayed publicly to users or included in ordinary application logs.

MasterLister does not need your eBay password as part of the normal supported authorization process.

  • operate Bulk Lister workflows;
  • manage Listing Bucket data;
  • create authorized eBay Seller Hub drafts;
  • manage connected eBay accounts;
  • apply subscription features and limits;
  • manage trials;
  • measure daily draft usage;
  • provide customer support;
  • maintain security;
  • investigate misuse;
  • maintain audit records;
  • improve service reliability.

  • billing cycle;
  • feature entitlements;
