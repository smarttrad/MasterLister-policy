Privacy Policy for Auto Draft Lister

Effective date: 15 September 2026
Operator: Surfmart Trading, Malaysia
Contact: surfmarttrading@gmail.com

1. About This Application

Auto Draft Lister is a private, locally operated application used by Surfmart Trading to prepare and manage draft listings for connected eBay seller accounts. It is not currently offered as a public software-as-a-service product.

Auto Draft Lister may be used with multiple eBay seller accounts owned or managed by Surfmart Trading. Each eBay account is authorized separately through eBay OAuth, and its tokens, settings, listing data, images, schedules, and draft records are kept separate from other connected accounts.

Auto Draft Lister is an independent application and is not sponsored, endorsed by, or affiliated with eBay Inc. eBay is a trademark of eBay Inc.

2. Information the Application Processes

When an eBay seller account is connected using eBay OAuth, the application may process only the information needed for its draft-listing functions, including:

  • The connected eBay account identifier and basic account identity information
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens
  • Seller account listing policies and related account configuration made available by eBay
  • Listing and draft information, including titles, categories, factual item specifics, prices, quantities, item locations, shipping settings, return-policy selections, and draft status
  • Excel data, product keywords, manually supplied competitor-reference URLs, schedule settings, and processing results
  • Local file paths and product images selected by the operator for upload to eBay
  • Public active-listing information returned through official eBay APIs
  • Limited technical logs needed to diagnose errors and prevent duplicate submissions

The current version does not request or process buyer order details, payment-card information, or the seller's eBay password.

The seller signs in directly on eBay's authorization page. Auto Draft Lister never receives the seller's eBay password.

3. How Information Is Obtained

Information is obtained from:

  • The operator, including Excel files, local images, manually selected reference URLs, and schedule settings
  • Official eBay APIs after the operator authorizes the relevant eBay seller account
  • Public active-listing information returned through official eBay APIs
  • Application-generated validation, scheduling, and draft-processing records

For Tool 1, the application performs competitor research automatically from product keywords in the operator's Excel workbook using official eBay APIs.

Until eBay grants access to an authorized sold-data API, Tool 1 automatic research is limited to active-listing and Best Match information. It is not represented as verified 30-day sold data.

For Tool 2, 30-day sold-product research is performed manually by the operator using eBay Seller Hub Product Research. The operator records selected competitor-reference URLs in a separate Tool 2 staging workbook.

The application does not scrape Seller Hub or automatically extract Product Research results.

Competitor-listing images and descriptions are not downloaded or copied. Draft listings use operator-supplied images, permitted eBay Catalog content where applicable, and descriptions created or approved by the operator.

4. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to:

  • Identify and maintain the correct connected eBay seller account
  • Research relevant active listings through official eBay APIs
  • Prepare and validate draft-listing information
  • Submit draft-listing data to eBay
  • Upload operator-selected product images to eBay
  • Apply account-specific policies, item locations, and listing schedules
  • Keep separate records for different Windows users and connected eBay accounts
  • Record the competitor-reference URL used for a draft
  • Troubleshoot failed or duplicate draft submissions

The application does not:

  • Sell or rent personal information
  • Use eBay data for unrelated advertising
  • Build advertising profiles
  • Publish or resell aggregated marketplace statistics
  • Scrape eBay websites or Seller Hub
  • Automatically publish listings without operator review

5. Local Storage and Account Separation

Application data is stored locally on the Windows computer operated by Surfmart Trading.

OAuth credentials must be protected using Windows security facilities or equivalent encrypted local storage. OAuth credentials must not be stored in Excel files or ordinary application logs.

Each Windows-user profile and each connected eBay account has a separate data area. Tokens, policies, item locations, workbooks, images, schedules, and draft records from one account must not be silently shared with another account.

The public GitHub page hosting this privacy policy does not receive the application's Excel files, images, OAuth tokens, or eBay account information.

6. Disclosure of Information

Information is sent to eBay only when necessary to:

  • Authorize a connected seller account
  • Retrieve permitted seller, catalog, category, or listing information
  • Upload operator-selected images
  • Create or manage draft listings

eBay processes this information under its own terms and privacy notice.

Surfmart Trading does not sell or rent the information. Information may be disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the application, its operator, eBay users, or legal rights.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

OAuth tokens are retained locally only while the relevant eBay account remains connected or until the tokens are revoked, deleted, invalidated, or no longer required.

Draft records, schedules, reference URLs, and technical logs are retained only while needed for the local listing workflow, troubleshooting, or a specific legal obligation.

Temporary image-staging files should be removed after processing when they are no longer needed. Original image files remain under the operator's control in the selected local folders.

To request disconnection and deletion of data associated with an eBay account, contact surfmarttrading@gmail.com.

After the account and request are verified, Surfmart Trading will revoke or remove the stored authorization where possible and delete the associated local application data, except information that must be retained for a specific legal obligation.

Before any Production use, Auto Draft Lister will implement and validate eBay's required marketplace account deletion or account closure notification process.

If eBay sends a valid account deletion notification after Production is enabled, data associated with that eBay account will be deleted or irreversibly anonymized as required, subject only to applicable legal retention obligations.

8. Security

Reasonable safeguards are used or planned for the local application, including:

  • Encrypted credential storage
  • Least-privilege OAuth access
  • Separation between Windows users and eBay accounts
  • Redaction of credentials and secrets from logs
  • Validation before draft submission
  • Duplicate-submission protection
  • Operator review in Seller Hub before publication

No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

The operator is responsible for securing the Windows accounts, computer, local folders, Excel files, and backups used with the application.

9. User Choices

The operator may:

  • Decline or revoke eBay authorization
  • Disconnect an eBay account
  • Stop using the application
  • Remove locally stored files
  • Request deletion using the contact address above

Revoking authorization may prevent the application from creating or managing drafts for that eBay account.

10. Children's Privacy

Auto Draft Lister is a business listing tool and is not directed to children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the application's actual data practices or legal requirements change.

The revised policy will be published at the same public URL with a new effective date. Materially different processing will not be described as active until it has been implemented.
