Compliance Policy

MasterLister is designed to assist users with legitimate eBay listing and listing-management workflows.

This Compliance Policy explains the standards users must follow when using MasterLister.

MasterLister is operated by Surfmart Trading.

Contact: surfmarttrading@gmail.com

1. User Responsibility

Users remain responsible for ensuring that their products, listings and account activities comply with:

  • applicable laws;
  • eBay policies;
  • intellectual property rules;
  • consumer-protection requirements;
  • product-safety requirements;
  • import/export requirements where applicable;
  • marketplace category restrictions.

MasterLister does not replace the user's compliance responsibilities.

2. eBay Policies

Users must comply with the applicable eBay User Agreement, seller policies, listing rules and other marketplace requirements.

Users must not use MasterLister to intentionally bypass:

  • eBay listing restrictions;
  • account limitations;
  • seller-performance rules;
  • marketplace enforcement;
  • API or automation restrictions;
  • product-category restrictions.

3. Intellectual Property

Users must respect intellectual property rights.

MasterLister must not be used to knowingly:

  • sell counterfeit goods;
  • list unauthorized replicas;
  • misuse trademarks;
  • reproduce copyrighted content without permission;
  • copy another seller's protected images;
  • copy another seller's protected descriptions;
  • falsely claim authorization from a brand.

Users are responsible for confirming that they have lawful rights to use the content and products they submit.

4. Product Images

Product images used through MasterLister should be:

  • owned by the user;
  • created by the user;
  • supplied by an authorized source;
  • licensed for the user's intended commercial use;
  • otherwise lawfully usable.

Do not intentionally copy competitor images without appropriate permission.

5. Product Research

Product Research must be used for legitimate market research and listing preparation.

Research information should be treated as reference data.

Users must independently verify information before publishing a listing.

MasterLister does not guarantee that third-party listing information is complete, accurate or current.

6. Listing Accuracy

Users must ensure listings accurately represent the product being sold.

Users should not intentionally provide:

  • misleading titles;
  • false brand information;
  • incorrect condition information;
  • deceptive product claims;
  • inaccurate quantities;
  • misleading images;
  • unsupported specifications.

7. Prohibited Products

Users must not knowingly use MasterLister to facilitate the sale of products that are unlawful or prohibited by the applicable marketplace.

This includes products restricted under applicable laws or eBay policies.

Users are responsible for checking whether a product may legally and contractually be listed in the relevant marketplace.

8. Counterfeit and Replica Goods

Counterfeit, infringing or unauthorized replica products must not be listed through MasterLister.

If a user cannot establish the lawful right to sell a branded or copyrighted product, they should not use MasterLister to create listings for that item.

9. Automation Use

MasterLister automation must be used responsibly.

Users must not intentionally use automation to:

  • overload third-party services;
  • evade marketplace rate limits;
  • circumvent account restrictions;
  • bypass subscription limits;
  • create abusive volumes of listings;
  • manipulate marketplace systems.

10. Daily Draft Limits

MasterLister may enforce daily Seller Hub draft limits.

These limits are intended to control use of the service and reduce excessive automated draft creation.

Users must not attempt to bypass draft limits by:

  • switching connected eBay accounts;
  • repeatedly reconnecting accounts;
  • creating duplicate MasterLister accounts;
  • manipulating client-side requests;
  • attempting unauthorized API access.

11. Connected Accounts

Users must only connect eBay accounts they are authorized to operate.

Users must not connect accounts belonging to another person or business without appropriate permission.

12. Data and Credentials

Users must not attempt to:

  • access another user's data;
  • obtain another user's eBay authorization credentials;
  • expose application secrets;
  • interfere with authentication;
  • bypass role or permission controls;
  • access Owner/Admin functions without authorization.

13. Administrative Access

MasterLister may use different roles such as:

  • User;
  • Admin;
  • Owner.

Administrative capabilities are restricted according to authorized roles and permissions.

Attempting to circumvent these controls may result in account suspension or termination.

14. Abuse and Fraud

MasterLister must not be used for:

  • fraud;
  • impersonation;
  • deceptive marketplace activity;
  • unlawful transactions;
  • account takeover;
  • unauthorized access;
  • distribution of knowingly false product information.

15. Enforcement

Where MasterLister identifies a significant compliance or security concern, the service may take reasonable action such as:

  • restricting a feature;
  • suspending access;
  • disconnecting affected functionality where necessary;
  • requesting clarification;
  • terminating an account in serious cases.

Actions should be proportionate to the issue and applicable legal obligations.

16. Reporting Compliance Concerns

Users may report suspected misuse, intellectual property concerns, security issues or other compliance matters to:

surfmarttrading@gmail.com

Please provide enough information for the issue to be reviewed.

17. Policy Updates

This Compliance Policy may be updated as:

  • MasterLister changes;
  • marketplace requirements change;
  • legal requirements change;
  • new features are introduced.

The current published version and effective date should remain available through MasterLister.
